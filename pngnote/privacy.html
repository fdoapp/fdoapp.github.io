PngNote privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app PngNote (com.github.fdoapp.pngnote)

PngNote collects no personal data.

It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It has no permission to use the network, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Your notes are files in a folder on your own device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your notes. Each page is a PNG image, each book is a folder, and titles, tags and backgrounds are kept in small text files (metadata.txt). They live in the folder you choose when you first start the app, and only there. PDFs you export are written to an Exports folder in the same place, and deleted items go to a hidden .trash folder there.
  • App settings. Which folder you chose, your pen and PDF settings and the screen sizes the app works out are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • The app does not back up its data to any cloud service (Android backup is switched off for the app).

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, record, sell or share personal information, location, contacts, identifiers or usage data.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not ask for the internet permission, so no part of the app, including the pen library it is built on, can connect to the internet.
  • It does not read your files outside the folder you chose. Access to that folder goes through Android's own folder picker.

Sharing

If you press Share, PngNote hands a PDF to the app you pick in Android's share sheet (for example your mail app or a cloud-sync app). From that moment the other app's own privacy policy applies. If you sync the notes folder with a service of your choice, that service's policy applies to those files. Neither happens unless you do it.

Installing through Google Play

If you install PngNote from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under Google's privacy policy, for example install statistics and, if you have turned it on, crash and performance data. The developer sees only the summaries that Google shows in Play Console. PngNote itself sends nothing to Google or to the developer.

Children

PngNote collects no data from anyone, including children, and has no accounts, chat or purchases.

Deleting your data

There is no data on any server to delete. Delete the notes folder to remove your notes; uninstall the app to remove its settings.

Open source

PngNote is free, open-source software under the MIT licence. You can read the code and check the statements above at github.com/fdoapp/PngNote.
